Roosevelt Island Tramway

A quick aerial trip across the East River with Manhattan views.

The Roosevelt Island Tramway is an aerial cable car that spans the East River, connecting Manhattan’s Upper East Side with Roosevelt Island.

About

Built: Opened in May 1976, it was the first commuter aerial tramway in the U.S. and carries passengers roughly 3,140ft across the river, offering unique skyline views of Manhattan's East Side, Roosevelt Island, and Queens.

Today: It functions as both a transit link and a tourist attraction accepting both MetroCards and OMNY for the fare. During operating times, the four-minute tram ride runs every 7.5–15 minutes between the Manhattan station at Second Avenue (59th–60th Street) and Roosevelt Island.

Directions

To board from Manhattan, go to the tram station at Second Avenue and East 60th Street.
